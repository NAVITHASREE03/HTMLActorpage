Vijay

ijay, popularly known as Thalapathy Vijay, is a famous Indian actor and politician who mainly works in Tamil cinema. His full name is Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar, and he was born on 22 June 1974 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. He started his acting career as a child artist and later became one of the leading actors in the Tamil film industry. He is well known for his excellent acting, dancing skills, action scenes and charming personality. Some of his popular movies include Ghilli, Thuppakki, Kaththi, Mersal, Master, Leo and The Greatest of All Time (GOAT). Apart from acting, Vijay is also known for his kindness, charity work and helping people in need. He started a political party called Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) in 2024. He has millions of fans who admire him for his hard work, confidence and dedication. He is popularly called Thalapathy by his fans and is considered one of the most influential personalities in Tamil cinema.
